# How to Automate Crafting

The **Crafting Pipe** gives you the possibility to automate crafting processes within your pipe network. 

## Setting the Recipe

1. Place a **Crafting Pipe** in your system.
2. Hold a **Wrench** and **Right-Click** the Crafting Pipe.
3. In the GUI that opens, you will be able to arrange items in a 3x3 crafting grid.
4. **Define the crafting recipe:** Place the exact recipe you want the pipe to craft inside the grid.

## How it works

Once the recipe is defined:
- All items going into this Crafting Pipe will be temporarily cached inside it.
- As soon as enough ingredients are stored inside to fulfill the recipe, the result item is automatically crafted.
- The newly crafted item will then be sent out through the pipe's output direction.

## Viewing Cache & Output Direction

While inside the Wrench GUI of the Crafting Pipe, you can also:
- See the currently **cached items** waiting inside the pipe to be used.
- Define the **output direction** where the crafted items will go.
